# LIRR fares and connections

The January 4, 2026 adult one-way matrix and station zones are recorded in `lirr_fares.json` and embedded in `index.html`, matching the existing local Metro-North fare approach. The source is https://www.mta.info/document/194866. General eligibility and transfer rules: https://www.mta.info/fares-tolls/lirr-metro-north.

Prices are integer cents. LIRR GTFS `peak_offpeak` determines the fare category; Metro-North clock rules are not applied. Intermediate fares outside Zones 1/3 have identical peak/off-peak amounts. Eligible NYC trips use CityTicket when cheaper. Far Rockaway uses the generally available standard fare, without assuming eligibility for the location-restricted Far Rockaway Ticket.

Same-direction LIRR transfers use one origin-to-destination fare. Direction-changing trips use the fixed adult via fares returned by MTA's dedicated `/fares` endpoint (Accept-Version: 3.0). The local snapshot includes 56 origin-zone/destination-zone/transfer-zone classes with both peak and off-peak prices. `viaSource.samples` records an actual station pair and named transfer for each class. Unmodified responses are saved in `lirr_via_fare_sources/`; re-import them with `python supporting_files/scripts/import_lirr_via_fares.py supporting_files/reference/lirr_via_fare_sources`. The importer rejects incomplete, noninteger, or conflicting prices. Prices are embedded in the site; displaying a fare makes no live fare-service request.

For an indirect detour with no published through-ticket class, the app prices two ordinary tickets and explicitly labels the total "Separate tickets". It does not present that total as a discounted via ticket. Normal via tickets appear as "One-way via [station]". Mineola–Valley Stream via Jamaica is $8 off-peak/$10.75 peak; Huntington–Ronkonkoma via Hicksville is $7.25.

Belmont Park and Elmont-UBS Arena use their published Zone 4 adult one-way fares and are not eligible for CityTicket. Mets-Willets Point uses Manhattan pricing, including the published Woodside direction-change exception. An off-peak inbound ticket to Mets is not stepped up because the onward Woodside train is peak. See the "Train to the Game" section of the MTA general fare page. Reduced fares, passes, onboard surcharges, and promotional discounts remain outside the adult one-way scope. The unsolicited TrainTime fallback link has been removed.

Eligible Metro-North/LIRR journeys via Grand Central offer a Combo Ticket using the ordinary off-peak origin-to-Grand-Central fare plus $8. Sources: https://www.mta.info/fares/combo-ticket and https://employee.lirr.org/BidSheets/2023/03.01.2023.pdf. The official general fare page confirms continuing eligibility; the original railroad notice documents the calculation. Combo Tickets cannot originate at Grand Central, Penn, or Harlem-125th Street, must be bought before boarding at the origin, and require location sharing in TrainTime. If two supported separate tickets cost less, show that total instead. The Combo Ticket total replaces individual leg prices; it is not added to them. Confirm the rule when updating railroad fares.

Grand Central Madison and Grand Central Terminal share canonical `GCT` for search, retaining operator-specific source stops. LIRR Penn maps to `NYP`. Grand Central cross-railroad connections allow 15 minutes and Penn LIRR/Amtrak connections allow 20 minutes; these are application defaults, not guaranteed connections. LIRR connections retain a five-minute floor and published station minimums. Searches remain limited to one transfer.

Live LIRR updates use the official `lirr%2Fgtfs-lirr` endpoint. A live sample on October 18, 2026 confirmed `TripDescriptor.trip_id` matches static `trip_id`; entity IDs append `_T`/`_V` and must not be treated as public train numbers. Match operator, trip, route, service date, and stop order. The shared railroad extension at field 1005 provides track data. Independent operator caches and settled fetch results preserve the other railroad when one feed fails; stale predictions are cleared.

Validation includes importer fixtures, `lirr.test.js`, and `lirr.browser.test.js`. The browser test requires Playwright and a local static server (`TEST_URL`, default port 8765). Browser validation found Penn/Amtrak and Grand Central connections, and a live smoke check matched 97 imported trips with 949 track assignments. Future feed counts naturally vary.
